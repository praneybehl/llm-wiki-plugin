# OpenAI

OpenAI is an artificial intelligence research company that builds and
deploys large generative models, including the GPT family such as
[[gpt-4]]. It began as a non-profit and later added a capped-profit arm.

## Research direction

The lab concentrates on scaling up general models rather than many small
task-specific ones, a bet reflected across its publications and product
releases.

## Notable people

Co-founders include [[ilya-sutskever]], who shaped its early research
agenda around large neural networks.

## Products

Its API exposes chat completions, embeddings, and image generation
endpoints that developers call over HTTP.
