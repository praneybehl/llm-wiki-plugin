# Ilya Sutskever

Ilya Sutskever is a machine learning researcher and a co-founder of
[[openai]], where he served as chief scientist. His early work on deep
neural networks helped kick off the modern era of the field.

## AlexNet

As a graduate student he co-authored AlexNet, the convolutional network
whose 2012 image-recognition win convinced the field that deep learning
scaled with data and compute.

## Sequence to sequence

He introduced the sequence-to-sequence framework that maps one variable
length sequence to another, an idea that fed directly into later
[[attention-mechanism]] research.

## Views on scaling

He is associated with the thesis that steadily enlarging models and
datasets keeps yielding capability gains, echoed in the
[[scaling-laws-paper]].
